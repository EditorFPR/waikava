# Waikava, 1876

An interactive companion to *The Bose vaka Turaga at Waikava: Seventeen Days on Vanua Levu, 22 November – 13 December 1876*, published by [Fiji Political Review](https://fijipoliticalreview.com).

**Live page:** https://editorfpr.github.io/waikava/

## What this is

A scrollable long-form piece tracing the voyage of HMS *Nymphe* from Levuka to Waikava in Cakaudrove for the third annual Bose vaka Turaga (Great Council of Chiefs) held under Sir Arthur Gordon, first Governor of Fiji. Includes an interactive SVG voyage map, a day-by-day timeline of the 17-day council, and an illustrated ship-and-islands section.

The reading piece is the FPR Portrait it accompanies. This page is the visual layer.

## Sources

- Sir Arthur Gordon (Lord Stanmore), *Fiji: Records of Private and of Public Life 1875-1880*, Vol. 2 (Edinburgh: R. and R. Clark, 1897), Chapter XII, "The Waikava Council and Native Councils in Fiji Generally," pp. 196-247.
- Museum of Archaeology and Anthropology, University of Cambridge (MAA): Dufty photographs of the Council, and the Cakaudrove gatu vakaviti (Z 3862, Z 30618) presented to Gordon by Tui Cakau on 12 December 1876.
- John Spurway, *Ma'afu, prince of Tonga, chief of Fiji* (Canberra: ANU Press, 2015), ch. 13.
